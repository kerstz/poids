Tally is a free, offline-first journal for what you eat and what you weigh.

• Food journal: meals, foods with portions, simple recipes, daily totals against your calorie goal.
• Weight: history and chart, kg / lb / st.
• Barcode scan: the camera reads EAN/UPC codes on the device. Products come from your own list first, then Open Food Facts (only the barcode or search words are sent, and you can turn this off).
• French Ciqual food table built in, searchable offline.
• Your data stays on your device. Back up to a ZIP file and restore it at any time; export weight and journal as CSV.
• No ads, no trackers, no account, no Google services.

Data: Open Food Facts contributors (ODbL), Anses Ciqual 2020 (Open Licence). Free software under the GNU GPL v3 or later.
