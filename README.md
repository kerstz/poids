# Tally

Track what you eat, what you weigh and how you move. On your phone, nowhere else.

> 🚧 **Work in progress.** Tally is still being built. Things may break or change.

> **Vibe coded.** Most of this project was written with AI coding assistants (Claude Code), guided by prompts rather than hand-written. Read the code before relying on it for anything sensitive.

## Why

Most nutrition apps want an account, your data and a subscription. Tally wants none of that: it's free, open source, works offline and keeps everything on your device.

## What it does

- Food diary with macros
- Barcode scanning
- Weight tracking
- Local backup, restore and CSV export

## Under the hood

Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room. No Google services, no trackers. Meant for F-Droid.
